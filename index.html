From advisory to merged patch

  1. Wrenpatch reads every lockfile

    Each new advisory from the GitHub Advisory Database and OSV is matched against the exact versions your repositories install.

  2. Wrenpatch traces the call path

    It follows imports to the vulnerable function. Code you never call is listed, then left alone.

  3. Wrenpatch opens a tested pull request

    The bump goes on its own branch, your test suite runs, and the pull request names the advisory and the call site.

Patches on your terms. You decide what merges and who hears about it.

Common questions

Does Wrenpatch merge without asking?

Only when you turn on merging for patch versions. Minor and major bumps always wait for a person.

How does Wrenpatch decide an advisory is reachable?

It follows imports from your entry points to the function the advisory names.

What access does Wrenpatch need?

Read access to manifests and lockfiles, and write access to open branches. It never reads secrets.

What does Wrenpatch not cover?

Container images and OS packages. Keep your image scanner for those.
